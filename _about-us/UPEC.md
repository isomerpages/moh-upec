<h3>Establishment of UPEC</h3>
<p>World-class healthcare must include a world-class pre-hospital emergency
care system. Pre-hospital emergency care (PEC) is the provision of emergency
medical care in a pre- or out-of-hospital setting, to an acutely ill or
injured patient.</p>
<p>It is a uniquely time-sensitive domain of healthcare where early recognition
and intervention have profound impact on patient outcome.</p>
<p>In February 2013, the Unit for Pre-hospital Emergency Care (UPEC) was
established by the Ministry of Health (MOH) to coordinate, monitor and
implement the various PEC strategies, as well as to provide clinical and
operational oversight.</p>
<p>Our vision is to establish a world-class PEC system, readily accessible
to all, and provide excellent patient outcomes.</p>
