<h1><strong>Policy&nbsp;&amp;&nbsp;Professional Standards</strong></h1>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/Coverpage_picture.jpeg">
</div>
<p>The Policy and Professional Standards team collaborates closely with the
Ministry of Health (MOH) to develop and implement service standards for
Emergency Ambulance Service (EAS) and Medical Transport Service (MTS),
ensuring compliance with the Healthcare Services Act (HCSA). These standards
require that EAS and MTS crews are properly trained, hold valid certifications,
and operate under the oversight of a Clinical Governance Officer (CGO)
who manages the clinical governance framework. Additionally, the team ensures
that EAS and MTS vehicles are properly equipped and maintained.</p>
<p>The team's initiatives include coordinating with training providers to
offer courses and workshops that enhance the skills of pre-hospital emergency
care (PEC) professionals. They also oversee the credentialing framework
for foreign-trained paramedics and EMTs, ensuring they meet HCSA requirements.
By working in close partnership with MOH, the team upholds high standards
of patient safety and care within the emergency medical services sector.</p>
