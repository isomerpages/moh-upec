<p><strong>Foreword by Clinical Director</strong>
</p>
<p>Our vision is for Singapore to possess a world-class PEC system, readily
accessible to all, and providing excellent patient outcomes. In order to
achieve this, we need to develop a coherent and viable framework for inter-agency
collaboration and coordination in the long-term development of PEC in Singapore,
promote public responsiveness in pre-hospital emergencies, strengthen Singapore’s
PEC Services to world-class standards and ensure that PEC is seamlessly
integrated into the health care system. We also aim to create a supportive
environment for research into PEC to improve health outcomes.</p>
<p>Let’s work together to improve PEC in Singapore!</p>
