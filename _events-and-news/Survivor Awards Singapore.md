<p>Every year, there were people who suffered an Out-of-Hospital Cardiac
Arrest (OHCA) in Singapore, a potentially lethal emergency if victims are
not attended to in time.&nbsp;Whether they are in a park, pool, or in the
privacy of their own home, the difference between life and death for many
of these people can be a matter of minutes and if a bystander takes action
to help them. To celebrate those who played a vital role in resuscitating
cardiac arrest victims, the Unit for Pre-hospital Emergency Care (UPEC)
will be holding the annual Survivor Awards Singapore ceremony to recognise
their outstanding efforts.</p>
<p>Survivors of cardiac arrest and their families will be invited to the
Survivor Awards Singapore ceremony to show their appreciation for those
who helped to resuscitate them. The rescuers who had performed bystander
CPR or defibrillation, dispatchers who had initiated the dispatcher-assisted
CPR and/or firefighters and paramedics involved in the resuscitation will
receive a certificate of commendation and appreciation for their life-saving
efforts.</p>
