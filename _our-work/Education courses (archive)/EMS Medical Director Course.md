<h1><strong>Emergency Medical Services (EMS) Medical Directors’ Course</strong></h1>

<p>The series of Emergency Medical Services (EMS) Medical Directors’ Course
are the localized version of the NAEMSP Medical Directors Course. It aims
to provide EMS knowledge and skills for participants who are required to
be a medical director or take up leadership role in the ambulance services.</p>
<p>The EMS Medical Directors’ Course can be divided into three sub-categories:</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>EMS Medical Directors’ Course – An entry course for medical directors
for both EAS and MTS. A prerequisite for the Advanced EMS Medical Directors’
Course.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Advanced EMS Medical Directors’ Course – This course provides advanced
knowledge for medical directors of EAS.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>EMS Leadership for SCDF Commanders – With the increasing demand of the
national EMS, firemen from SCDF are cross-trained as EMTs. This course
offers commanders from SCDF an oversight of EMS.</p>
</li>
</ol>
