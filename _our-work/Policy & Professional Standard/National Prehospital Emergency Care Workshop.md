<p>The Ministry of Health (MOH), in collaboration with the Institute of Technical
Education (ITE), has established a national initiative to enhance the competencies
of paramedics and other pre-hospital emergency care professionals. Recognizing
the critical role that paramedics play in leading emergency ambulance teams
and providing care to patients in pre-hospital settings, this initiative
aims to provide continuous training and skill development.</p>
<p>ITE College East, a key education provider for paramedicine in Singapore's
civilian sector, has been engaged by MOH to conduct continuing professional
development (CPD) and education programs for paramedics, Emergency Medical
Technicians (EMTs) and other pre-hospital emergency care professionals.
This joint effort is part of a broader national strategy to strengthen
pre-hospital emergency care by ensuring that these professionals are equipped
with the latest knowledge and skills. The collaboration underscores the
commitment of MOH, UPEC, and ITE to improve the quality and effectiveness
of emergency medical services through ongoing professional development.</p>
